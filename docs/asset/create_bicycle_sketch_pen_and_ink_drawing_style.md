# Architectural Sketch of a Modern Road Bicycle

A detailed, black-and-white industrial design sketch of a modern road or gravel bicycle, rendered in a technical pen-and-ink drafting style with cross-hatching, fine line work, and visible construction lines.

---

## 1. Composition & Perspective
* **Viewpoint:** Three-quarters front-right perspective, angled towards the right foreground.
* **Background:** Clean, solid off-white background with subtle architectural layout grids and perspective guidelines beneath the tires.

---

## 2. Bicycle Components

### Frame & Fork
* **Frame:** Classic diamond-geometry frame featuring streamlined tubing (top tube, down tube, seat tube, chainstays, and seatstays).
* **Fork:** Rigid, aero-profile front fork connecting directly to the front wheel hub.
* **Mounts & Hardware:** Visible mounting eyelets (for bottle cages) on the down tube and seat tube.

### Cockpit & Controls
* **Handlebars:** Ergonomic drop handlebars wrapped in textured handlebar tape.
* **Levers:** Dual-control integrated brake and shift levers (brifters) mounted on the handlebar hoods.
* **Stem & Headset:** Threadless stem clamping the center of the handlebars to the fork steerer tube.
* **Cabling:** Internal/semi-external control cables arcing neatly from the levers into the frame.

### Wheels & Brakes
* **Wheels:** Deep-section road rims laced with detailed wire spokes attached to central hubs.
* **Tires:** Narrow, smooth-tread road tires with precise contour shading.
* **Braking System:** Modern disc brake rotors and calipers visible at the center of the front and rear wheel hubs.

### Drivetrain & Gearing
* **Crankset:** Two-piece crank arm with dual front chainrings (2x setup).
* **Rear Drivetrain:** Multi-speed cassette attached to the rear hub, connected via a roller chain to a low-profile rear derailleur.
* **Front Derailleur:** Compact front derailleur clamped onto the lower section of the seat tube.

### Saddle & Seating
* **Saddle:** Streamlined, contoured racing saddle with hollow rail mounts underneath.
* **Seatpost:** Cylindrical seatpost secured into the seat tube via a dedicated seat clamp.

---

## 3. Artistic & Technical Details
* **Line Art:** Precise ink contours combined with cross-hatching to define metallic reflections, cylindrical curves, and ambient shadows.
* **Drafting Elements:** Visible intersection ticks, alignment crosses, and extended guideline strokes around major junction points (head tube, bottom bracket, dropouts).
* **Ground Shadow:** Minimalist horizontal and angled cross-hatch strokes beneath both tires establishing contact with a flat ground plane.

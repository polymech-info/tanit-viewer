# Tanit in Chrome

Open Tanit beside any page and get things done without switching context.

- Search X/Twitter trends and summarize what people are talking about.
- Research a topic across several pages and compare the findings.
- Fill in forms from documents or files already on your PC.
- Translate a page, a reply, or a selected passage into another language.
- Talk to Tanit in real time while you browse.
- Ask questions about the page in front of you.
- Point at a chart, table, image, or paragraph and use just that part.
- Compare an online product, specification, or offer with local documents.
- Turn a video into a transcript or subtitles.
- Translate subtitles and create a dubbed version.
- Capture an image from a page, inspect it, resize it, or save it.
- Summarize long articles, manuals, reports, or discussions.
- Extract tables, dates, prices, contacts, or structured information.
- Draft a reply and place it into the right field.
- Navigate through multi-step forms and websites.
- Save useful results into local files.
- Run a Tanit command or workflow directly from the page.

## Fast with your voice

You do not need to describe everything precisely.

Say:

> “Find the latest discussions about this product and give me the common complaints.”

> “Compare these three offers with the PDF on my desktop.”

> “Fill this form from the invoice, but ask before submitting.”

> “Translate this reply into French and put it here.”

> “What does this warning mean?”

> “Make English subtitles for this video.”

> “Now create a Spanish dubbed version.”

Tanit can combine the page, your voice, local files, and its native tools in the
same task.

## Research without tab chaos

Start with a question instead of manually opening twenty tabs.

Tanit can search, open results, read pages, follow relevant links, compare
sources, and keep the useful parts together.

Use it for:

- trends and social discussions;
- technical research;
- product comparisons;
- documentation;
- travel and local research;
- news and current topics;
- troubleshooting;
- market and competitor research.

Ask follow-up questions naturally while the research stays in context.

## Forms without copy and paste

Give Tanit the source material and let it do the boring part.

A PDF, invoice, spreadsheet, text file, previous result, or local folder can
supply the information needed by a web form.

Tanit can identify fields, enter values, choose options, move between pages,
and stop when something needs your decision.

Fast when it is routine. Careful when it matters.

## Any language, in place

Read a page in another language without leaving it.

Translate one sentence or the whole useful section. Dictate in your own
language and answer in another. Listen to selected text. Compare the translated
version with the original.

The same works for documents, subtitles, recordings, and videos.

## From video to subtitles or dubbing

Found a useful video?

Go straight from:

`video → transcript → subtitles`

or:

`video → transcript → translation → dubbed version`

Use local or configured speech, translation, and voice tools without building a
separate workflow in another application.

## Point, ask, act

Point at the exact thing you mean:

- a paragraph;
- a form field;
- a button;
- a chart;
- a product image;
- a table;
- part of a page.

Then ask what you want done with it.

Read it. Explain it. Translate it. Save it. Compare it. Speak it. Use it in the
next step.

## The web is only the starting point

A task can begin in Chrome and continue anywhere Tanit can work.

Search the web, use a local file, process an image, transcribe audio, run a
command, create subtitles, talk by voice, or continue as an XBlox workflow.

**Start with the page. Finish the job.**

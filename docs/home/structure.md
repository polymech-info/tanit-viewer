# Key differentiators to emphasize

Avoid marketing the product as "yet another ChatGPT desktop app." Instead, repeatedly reinforce what makes it distinct:

* **AI that works with your desktop** — files, terminals, browsers, Office documents, Git, SSH, and local applications.
* **Local-first architecture** — choose local or cloud models, or combine both.
* **Native performance** — C++ core with a modern React/WebView interface.
* **Extensible by design** — plugins, tools, workflows, and agents built on a single platform.
* **Security by default** — sandboxed execution, explicit permissions, signed plugins, and enterprise policy support.
* **Built for serious work** — handling large codebases, engineering documents, media, and automation rather than simple chat.

This positioning is much harder for competitors to copy because it focuses on the **desktop AI platform** you've been designing, rather than the underlying language model itself.

### One suggestion that fits your product especially well

Instead of polished feature tours, make each video feel like someone solving a **real job** in under five minutes.

For example:

* **"My CNC machine keeps failing. Find the cause."** (PDF manuals, photos, logs, browser, voice)
* **"Migrate this 2-million-line codebase."** (code, terminal, Git, AI)
* **"Process 5,000 product photos before lunch."** (images, metadata, batch automation)
* **"Turn three hours of meetings into a project plan."** (audio, transcription, documents)
* **"My AI joins me while I work."** (voice, screen awareness, desktop integration)

Those stories are memorable because viewers can immediately map them onto their own work, even if their profession is completely different. The product becomes the capable assistant helping them get through a real task, rather than a collection of AI features.
